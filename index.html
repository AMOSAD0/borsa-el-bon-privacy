Privacy Policy – Borsa El Bon

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how the Borsa El Bon app ("the App"), provided by Ahmed Mosad ("we"), handles information when you use it.

1. Information we collect

The current version of the App does not require an account or sign-in. It does not collect any personal data, such as your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

2. Information stored only on your device

The App saves a few settings on your device to make it easier to use:

  • The app language (Arabic or English).
  • The appearance (light or dark).
  • Whether you have seen the introduction screens on first launch.

These settings stay on your device, are not sent to us, and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Connecting to our servers

The App connects to the internet to show content such as news, articles, categories and offers. Each request includes your chosen language so the content arrives in that language. Like any internet service, our servers also log temporary technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request. We use this only to run and protect the service, not to identify you.

All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

4. Ads and tracking

The App shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

5. Sharing information

We do not sell or share any information with third parties.

6. Permissions

The App asks for only one permission, internet access, to load its content.

7. Children

The App is intended for people working in the coffee trade, not for anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add features such as sign-in. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact us

For any privacy questions: mark2michil@gmail.com
